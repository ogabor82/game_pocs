# Word Potions

Magic Sort–style (water sort) puzzle for English vocabulary (A1–A2). Every vial holds pearl liquid layers with English words; pour the top word onto a word of the same meaning group, or into an empty vial. All layers look the same until a vial holds the 4 words of one group — then it lights up in that group's colour and gets corked. 5 levels, the last one hides the lower words until they surface.

Open `index.html` in a browser — no build step. Levels live in the `LEVELS` table at the top of the script; each layout is shuffled and checked by a solver before play.
